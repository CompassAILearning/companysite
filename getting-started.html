Getting Started

Your personalized study plan in three simple steps

Setting up CourseCadence takes about 10 minutes. Have your syllabi handy and we'll handle the rest.

  1. Step 1: Upload your class syllabus

    Upload a PDF, Word document or photo of each syllabus. CourseCadence pulls out every assignment, reading, quiz and exam date automatically.

    • Supports PDF, DOCX and image uploads
    • Review and edit anything we extract before it goes on your calendar
    • Add courses at any point in the term

  2. Step 2: Answer questions about your schedule and learning style

    Tell us how you study best and what else is on your plate. A short questionnaire covers things like:

    • When you focus best: mornings, afternoons or late nights
    • How long you like to study in one sitting
    • Jobs, sports, clubs and other commitments to plan around

  3. Step 3: Get your personalized study plan

    CourseCadence builds a week-by-week plan with study blocks, reading time and exam prep, and sends push notifications so nothing slips.

    • Plans adjust automatically when you miss a session or add a deadline
    • Sync with Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar
    • Check in daily to see exactly what's next

What you'll need

  • A syllabus for each course you're taking this term
  • Your weekly class schedule
  • Rough hours for work, practice or other commitments
  • A campus or personal email to create your account

Frequently asked questions

How much does CourseCadence cost?

Every account starts with a free 30-day trial. Pricing details for after the trial will be announced soon.

Do I need a campus email to sign up?

No. You can sign up with a campus or personal email address.

What if my syllabus changes mid-semester?

Upload the new version or edit dates directly. Your plan rebalances automatically around the changes.

Does CourseCadence work for graduate students?

Yes. Anyone managing coursework and deadlines can use CourseCadence, from first-year undergrads to grad students.

Is my data private?

Yes. We only use your information to build your plan, and we never sell student data. See our Privacy Policy and Campus Data Security pages for details.
