About CompassAI

We're building the college companion we wish we'd had

CompassAI creates tools that help students handle the jump to college. CourseCadence, our first product, turns a stack of syllabi into one personalized plan so students can focus on learning instead of logistics.

Our mission

College asks students to manage more at once than ever before: several courses, each with its own syllabus, deadlines and expectations, along with jobs, sports, family and a social life.

We believe no student should have to work all of that out alone. Our mission is to give every student a calm, clear view of what's next and a plan that adapts when life happens.

We design with students, not just for them. Every feature starts from real conversations about what makes college hard.

What we believe

The principles behind every decision we make about CourseCadence.

Students first

We build for the student's long-term success, not for screen time. A good day in CourseCadence is one where you open it, know what to do and get back to your life.

Flexible, not rigid

Plans should bend when life happens. Missed a session? CourseCadence reworks the rest of your week instead of making you feel behind.

Privacy by default

Your schedule and coursework belong to you. We collect only what we need to build your plan and never sell student data.
